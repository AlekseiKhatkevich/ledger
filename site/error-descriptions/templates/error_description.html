out-of-credit

The account does not have sufficient credit to complete the requested operation.

Short details

Summary
Insufficient funds on customer's account.
Suggested status
403 (Forbidden)
Severity
Medium

Human-readable description

The request cannot be completed because the customer's balance is lower than the required amount. Inform the user of their current balance and provide steps to top up or retry with a different payment method.

Actionable guidance

  • Show current balance and the required amount.
  • Offer actions: "Add funds", "Use different payment method", "Contact support".
  • Log this event with account ID and attempted charge amount.
